Michi Michi was founded in 2020 as a bakery co-op: a collective of local San Diego bakers rather than a traditional owner-operated bakery. Bakers set their own retail prices, and each week receive 70% of gross sales, while Michi Michi retains 30% to support operations. Every baker in the co-op has a stake in the business, the menu, and the quality of what comes out of the kitchen each day.

Michi Michi is always open to connecting with talented bakers who believe in doing things the right way and are not afraid to get creative. If you are interested in joining the co-op or being considered for a guest residency, send us an email at hello@michimichibakery.com. Tell us about yourself, your background, what you make, and why Michi Michi feels like the right fit. We read every single inquiry and while we cannot always respond immediately, the right opportunities have a way of coming together.

The Michi Michi residency program is our way of bringing fresh creative energy into the kitchen on a seasonal basis. Each residency is a chance for a talented baker to design and run their own seasonal menu under the Michi Michi roof, with the full support of our co-op behind them. Think of it as a creative takeover with really good infrastructure.

Residencies are seasonal, typically eight to twelve weeks, and we select bakers based on their craft, their creative point of view, and their fit with the Michi Michi community. To be considered, email hello@michimichibakery.com with your background, examples of your work, and a short pitch for what your residency menu might look like. We want to see how you think, not just what you can bake.
