Our Menu

Our menu is a mix of seasonal pastries and a small collection of classics we always keep around. Everything is baked fresh in limited batches, so what’s available will change throughout the day.

We don’t offer online ordering, but we promise it’s worth the visit. Come by, and we’ll have something warm waiting for you.
