Our Community

At Michi Michi Bakery, we are more than just a bakery; we are a vibrant community hub. We believe in fostering connections and supporting local initiatives that make a positive impact on our neighborhood. From hosting community events to partnering with local charities, we are committed to giving back and creating a welcoming space for everyone.

Guest Chef Residency

A rotating showcase of local bakers, chefs, and culinary artists.

Each season, we invite a guest baker to take over part of the co-op kitchen and share their craft with the community. From regional techniques to experimental flavors, every residency brings new stories, new perspectives, and limited-time bakes you won’t find anywhere else.

Summer 2026: Chef Julien Demonchy

This summer, we welcome Chef Julien Demonchy from Montréal for a three-month residency exploring neo-Parisian pastry, modern viennoiserie, and intricate laminated doughs inspired by French tradition and contemporary technique.

Upcoming Events

Workshops, pop-ups, and gatherings for the baking community.

Join us for our upcoming events and be a part of the Michi Michi community! From baking workshops to charity fundraisers, there's always something exciting happening at our bakery. Check out the details below and mark your calendars!

Sourdough Starter Workshop

Learn how to build, feed, and bake with your own wild yeast starter in this hands-on beginner workshop.

Summer Pizza Night Pop-up

For one night only, our bakers fire up the ovens for seasonal pizzas, soft serve, and late-night desserts inspired by California summers.

Celebrate peak summer produce with an evening tasting featuring apricot danishes, cherry galettes, peach hand pies, and other seasonal bakes.
