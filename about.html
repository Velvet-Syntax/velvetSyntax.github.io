Our Story

Michi Michi was created as a space for connection through food—where ideas, cultures, and people come together at the table. What began as a simple vision has grown into a platform for chefs to create, collaborate, and share their stories. By welcoming resident chefs from different backgrounds, Michi Michi offers an ever-evolving dining experience shaped by diverse perspectives and traditions. Each residency brings something new, inviting guests to explore flavors, techniques, and narratives from around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster creativity and cultural exchange through food. We aim to support chefs by providing a space to experiment, collaborate, and showcase their craft while building meaningful connections with the community. Through thoughtful dining experiences, we strive to celebrate diversity, spark curiosity, and create moments that bring people together.
